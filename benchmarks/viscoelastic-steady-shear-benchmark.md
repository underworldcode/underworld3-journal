Family: [Benchmarks](../benchmarks/benchmarks.md) / viscoelastic. Equations for the models: [Viscoelastic Constitutive models](../topics/viscoelastic-constitutive-models.md).

## The problem

A Couette box: unit gap, walls moving at $\pm 0.5$ so the shear rate $\dot\gamma = 1$, $\eta_p = G = 1$ so $\lambda = 1$ and $\mathrm{Wi} = \lambda\dot\gamma = 1$. Run to a steady state; read the stress at the centre by projection. The closed forms:

Oldroyd-B (and UCM):

$$
\tau_{xy} = \eta_p\dot\gamma, \qquad N_1 = \tau_{xx} - \tau_{yy} = 2G\,\mathrm{Wi}^2 .
$$

FENE-P in two dimensions, with $c_{yy} = 1/f$, $c_{xy} = \mathrm{Wi}/f^2$, $c_{xx} = (1 + 2\mathrm{Wi}^2/f^2)/f$ and the closure giving one cubic for the spring factor:

$$
f^2 (f - 1) = \frac{2\,\mathrm{Wi}^2}{L^2}, \qquad \tau_{xy} = \frac{\eta_p\dot\gamma}{f}, \qquad N_1 = \frac{2G\,\mathrm{Wi}^2}{f^2} .
$$

At $\mathrm{Wi} = 1$, $L^2 = 10$: $f = 1.1510$, $\tau_{xy} = 0.8688$, $N_1 = 1.5097$.

## What the references report

The closed forms are exact; any steady-state error is the time discretisation of the step.

## Our status (2026-10-05)

| quantity | reference | ours |
|---|---|---|
| Oldroyd-B $\tau_{xy}$, $N_1$ | 1, 2 | within 0.03 and 0.08 at $\Delta t = 0.1$; every history agrees |
| FENE-P, infinite $L^2$ | Oldroyd-B | equal to 1e-8 |
| FENE-P $\tau_{xy}$ error at $\Delta t$ 0.1, 0.05, 0.025 | 0 | +0.0068, +0.0034, +0.0018 (first order) |
| FENE-P $N_1$ error at the same steps | 0 | +0.0094, +0.0051, +0.0027 |

## What we claim and do not claim

We claim the step is consistent and first order for both relaxation laws, with the log store and the log(f c) record. We do not claim second order: the positive-definite path is first order by construction.

## What did not work

An early form of the FENE-P step kept one $1/f$ too many on the stretching source and sat 0.03 and 0.13 off the closed form at every $\Delta t$: an error that does not shrink with $\Delta t$ is a derivation error, not a discretisation error. This benchmark caught it.
