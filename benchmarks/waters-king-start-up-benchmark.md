Family: [Benchmarks](../benchmarks/benchmarks.md) / viscoelastic. Methods: [Transport histories](../topics/transport-histories.md).

## The problem

Start-up of plane channel flow of an Oldroyd-B fluid from rest under a suddenly imposed pressure gradient (Waters and King 1970). The velocity overshoots its steady value and rings down at a rate set by $\mathrm{Wi}$ and $\beta$; the analytic solution is a series in the eigenmodes of the channel. Because the flow is unidirectional and the stress is advected along streamlines that stay put, it isolates the time integrator (the overshoot peak) from the transport (the settled state, which a history must hold without drift).

## What the references report

The analytic transient: peak velocity, its time, and the decay of the oscillation.

## Our status (2026-09-16)

| history | behaviour as $\Delta t$ falls |
|---|---|
| backward from the nodes | converges to the analytic transient |
| backward from the integration points | rings, then diverges as $\Delta t$ falls (the memory term amplifies the low-Courant mode; store smoothing holds it) |
| Eulerian (SUPG + DEVSS) | diverges at every $\Delta t$ (#749) |

## What we claim and do not claim

We claim it separates the histories: the peak tests the integrator, the settled state tests the transport. We do not yet have a figure on this page; the runs predate the wiki.

## Next target

Rerun with the forward histories and the global projection, and plot the transient against the series for every history on one figure.
