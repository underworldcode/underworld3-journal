## The problem

Two opposed inflows meet two opposed outflows in a cross-shaped channel; the centre is a stagnation point of planar extension. For an upper-convected Maxwell or Oldroyd-B fluid in creeping flow the symmetric steady state loses stability at a critical Deborah number and the flow takes one of two asymmetric states, a pitchfork bifurcation with no inertia (Poole, Alves and Oliveira 2007). An order parameter measures the asymmetry, for example the imbalance of the flow rate leaving through each outlet from one inlet.

## What the references report

The critical Deborah number of the pitchfork: 0.35 in the definition we used (Poole et al.).

## Our status (2026-09-25)

| quantity | reference | ours |
|---|---|---|
| pitchfork appears | yes | only with the log store, the exponential read and the positive-definite update; the upper-convected BDF1 drops the $\Delta t^2 \mathbf{L}\mathbf{c}^*\mathbf{L}^T$ term and does not show it |
| critical De | 0.35 | about 0.71, a factor two high |

## What did not work

The first order parameter (a DQ flux imbalance) was blind to the asymmetry. Diagnostics read by `evaluate` instead of by projection misled us. Seeding with a swirl body force is what made the bifurcation visible.

## Next target

Parked behind the cylinder work: find the factor two (definition of De, resolution at the stagnation point, the store).
