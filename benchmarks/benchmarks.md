## What we learned about benchmarking itself

- Look at the frame, check it has saturated, then tabulate. Three comparisons lied in one afternoon before we made that a rule.
- A test asserts against a hard baseline or it is not a test.
- A metric needs a fixture that shows the effect: a regularisation checked only on a stable run hid an 18% diffusion.
- A benchmark is a page of its own, inside a family. A flat list of every benchmark one after another stopped working at the second family.
