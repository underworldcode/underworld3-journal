## What was learned

- **The faulted-mesh rebuild leaked 7 GB per rebuild, and the leak is fixed.** Strong
  references in the default Model's mesh and solver registries, SymPy caches holding
  coordinate symbols, mesh variables keeping their constructor arguments, and
  petsc4py never running its collective cleanup in parallel. Branch
  `bugfix/faulted-rebuild-leak` (547c2e54, hyperion); validated on kaiju at 16 ranks
  over 30 steps: slip identical to 6e-10, resident memory flat, time unchanged. A
  300-step faulted run no longer needs memory segments. The fix touches registries,
  caches and PETSc cleanup, so it goes to a PR against `development` after the
  adversarial review; that is now the priority.
- **A restart that lands on a rebuild differs from the uninterrupted run by a factor
  two in peak slip at that step, and it is not the driver.** The two overlays agree to
  6e-10; the difference is the Stokes solver's own state, which a restart cannot
  rebuild (about 5e-4 in vrms), and it shrinks over the following steps. Recorded as a
  known limitation: compare across a restart in vrms, events and trench history, not
  in single-step peak slip.
- **The warm-start switch after a fault rebuild was relative to the wrong residual.**
  `rotated_bc.py` switched Picard to Newton at a tolerance relative to the solve's own
  starting residual, so a good warm start still ran 20 to 37 Picard iterations.
  Fixed on `bugfix/warm-start-newton-switch` (371d3bd): the 3582 same-set rebuild
  drops from 27 to 19 nonlinear iterations. A band probe showed the remaining warm
  residual sits entirely within 0.08 of the old and new fault traces, so the next
  lever is a fault-attached transfer, not the switch.
- **The campaign does not scale past one node yet.** At 32 ranks and above the
  custom-prolongation multigrid fails to build (a PETSc preallocation error in the
  nested interpolation) and falls back; at 104 ranks an empty coarse rank raised and
  then hung. The empty-rank fix (`bugfix/custom-mg-empty-rank`) does not unblock 32
  ranks: that is a second bug, with the coarse-solve design, on hyperion. Sixteen
  ranks is the working point (99 s per ordinary step, efficiency 0.75 against eight).
- **Crust particle sampling is particles per cell, not a material density.**
  `-uw_crust_ppc` and `-uw_crust_refill_every` seed the crust layer densely; both off
  by default. The uniform-swarm thermal runs carry a broken weak crust because of the
  proxy, which this fixes.
