What we understand about carrying a stress or a field along the flow: the semi-Lagrangian histories, the Eulerian path, and which to use where. Usage: `docs/developer/subsystems/stress-transport.md`, `docs/developer/guides/transport-schemes.md`.

## The flavours

Four semi-Lagrangian histories, named by trace direction and launch locus: backward or forward in time, from the nodes or from the integration points. Plus the Eulerian path with SUPG stabilisation and DEVSS for the stress. The composed Navier–Stokes and advection–diffusion solvers take the momentum history, the field history and the stress history as independent choices (`velocity_transport`, `transport`, `stress_transport`), and all of them run the Maxwell shear box to the same answer, serial equal to parallel to 1e-6 at three or more ranks (two ranks is a special case and proves nothing).

## What each is good at

- **Backward from the nodes** converges on Waters–King start-up shear and is the reference for a transient.
- **Backward from the integration points** rings at cell scale through the store cycle; store smoothing α = c h² with c 0.03 to 0.07 holds it. It runs at Courant one, not below: the memory term amplifies the low-Courant mode, so refine h and dt together.
- **Forward (either launch)** reaches a given cylinder answer about four times coarser than the Eulerian path, once its arrivals are read back by the global projection (see [Particle-to-field projection](../topics/particle-to-field-projection.md)). The forward flavours are first order.
- **Eulerian SUPG/DEVSS** diverges on Waters–King at every time step and under-resolves the stress sheets on a cylinder's shear layers (its lift falls at res 80 where the forward family has settled), but it gives the cleanest Strouhal number at coarse resolution.

Two findings from the cylinder that hold across schemes: the **Strouhal number follows the velocity history** (SUPG 0.30, forward-nodes 0.25 to 0.28 whatever carries the stress), and at Re 200 the **lift collapses with resolution in every scheme**, so coarse "nice vortices" are an artefact.

## What did not work, and why

- **The nodal trace-back next to a no-slip wall** carries about 50% too much stress in the first two cells: it reads from where the wall value is pinned. Grid and integration-point histories converge together there.
- **The particle (Lagrangian) history** read unrelated rows after a removal (PETSc's swap-with-last on repopulation); the proxy hid an error of order one at the particle level. Look at particle-level error before blaming a method.
- **Partition dependence** came from five separate sources (loose projection tolerance, a nudged record, a nudged trace start, stranded points falling back to RBF, cell tie-breaks). Each had to be found on its own.
- **Seeding tracers on the centreline** of the cylinder lost them all at the wall (#810).
